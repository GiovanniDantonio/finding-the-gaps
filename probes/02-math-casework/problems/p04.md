The vertices of a 7-cycle are colored with colors from a palette of 4 colors so that adjacent vertices get different colors. How many such colorings use exactly 3 of the 4 colors? Vertices are labeled, so rotations and reflections count as different colorings.

Give the final answer as a single integer, and nothing else, in the answer field. In the reasoning field, lay out the cases you split on and the count for each case.
